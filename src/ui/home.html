Plan an experiment

Choose your lab and the equipment you use, list what the experiment needs (plastics, gloves, machine hours), and see its CO₂ and greener swaps approved by lab experts.

  • Covers consumables, where most lab emissions come from
  • An AI can choose and explain the swaps; a calculator makes every number

Book equipment

Book sessions on lab equipment and see if moving them to a cleaner hour of the electricity grid, sharing a device or joining a run would lower their CO₂.

  • Electricity only (TU/e buys green electricity, so savings are small)
  • Optional AI: it chooses among the moves the rules allow
