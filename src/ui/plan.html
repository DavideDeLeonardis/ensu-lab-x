New here? What this page does and what to try
  1. Your plan is a shopping list for one week of a (fictional) lab experiment: disposable plastic items, gloves, and hours on two machines. Hover an item for a plain explanation. Your changes are kept when you reload the page; "Reset to demo plan" starts over.
  2. Estimate shows the CO₂ of that list. Plastics and gloves count from production; machines count their electricity. The range (low–high) shows how uncertain the numbers are.
  3. Suggestions (at most 3) are swaps that lab experts approved in advance, such as washable glass instead of throw-away plastic. The AI only chooses among those swaps and writes the text; it never makes up numbers.
  4. Expected for the demo plan: usually swaps for the plastic droppers, the plastic tubes and the large clean workbench (use the small one: less electricity). With "Green contract" the workbench swap saves nothing, so the pipette tips take its place. The bioreactor is not touched (fixed by the protocol), and neither are the 20 dishes for growing cells (they need the special tc_treated surface glass lacks).
  5. The estimate updates by itself as you edit. Ask the AI lets the AI choose among the same swaps and write the text (~10 s). It reads your "Notes for the AI", which the rules cannot: it may drop a swap only by quoting your notes (checked), and the page shows what it changed compared with the rules. In our tests only Claude understood the notes; the small local model keeps your data on this computer but just rewrites the text. Machine rows show the hours already booked in the booking assistant ("Use 12 h" copies them).
  6. Try: tick tc_treated on the 30 dishes → no glass swap for them. Tick locked on the droppers and write a reason → that suggestion disappears. Switch the estimate to "Green contract" → electricity drops to 0 and plastics dominate.
  7. How do I know it worked? (below the suggestions, open it) re-checks the answer and shows a ✓ for each rule.
