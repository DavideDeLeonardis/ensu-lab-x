Method. Estimate per booking = Σ over 15-min steps of (equipment power × grid intensity at that hour). Confocal sessions include a 30-min laser warm-up; autoclave cycles count 6 kWh regardless of load; incubator shelves carry a pro-rata share of the unit's daily energy. The range applies each device's low/high factor. Always-on devices (freezers, heat block, water bath) are shown on the plan but are not booked. Researcher IDs are pseudonyms.
Grid profile. Synthetic hourly curve shaped on Electricity Maps' 2025 review for the Netherlands (annual mean 262 g/kWh, midday solar trough, evening peak near 20:00). Replace with a real hourly export before the final demo.
